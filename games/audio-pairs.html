How to play

  • Start a round from the game menu.
  • Listen to the spoken French prompt.
  • Choose the matching written option.
  • Keep going to improve listening and recall speed.

Available settings

  • Speech: enable/disable speech and adjust playback speed.
  • Auto-play on Reveal: useful for repeated listening practice.
  • Sound Effects: toggle right/wrong feedback sounds.

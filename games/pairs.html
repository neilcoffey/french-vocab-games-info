How to play

  • Tap two cards to reveal them.
  • Find matching translation pairs.
  • Matched cards stay open; unmatched cards flip back.
  • Clear all pairs to complete the round.

Available settings

  • Board Size: 4, 6, or 8 pairs.
  • Sound Effects: toggle match/mismatch feedback sounds.

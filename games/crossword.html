How to play

  • Read an English clue from the crossword list.
  • Enter the matching French word in the grid.
  • Use intersecting letters to help solve harder entries.
  • Complete the full board to finish the round.

Available settings

  • Layout: choose crossword layout style (English/French).
  • Speech: optional pronunciation playback where clips are available.
  • Sound Effects: toggle feedback sounds.

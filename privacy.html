Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Overview

French Vocab Games is designed to work without creating an account. The app stores gameplay data on your device to support settings, saved progress, and learning statistics.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information directly from users inside the app. No name, email address, phone number, or precise location is required to use the app.

Data Stored on Device

The app may store local data such as vocabulary progress, game history, and preferences. This data remains on your device unless you choose to back up your device through Apple services.

Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

French Vocab Games does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not track your activity across apps or websites.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share locally stored gameplay data with third parties.

Children's Privacy

French Vocab Games is a general-audience educational app and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

How to play

  • Reveal a card prompt and think of the translation.
  • Flip to check the answer.
  • Mark your result and continue through the round.
  • Repeat regularly to build long-term recall.

Available settings

  • Game Length: 10, 20, or 30 items per round.
  • Speech: optional pronunciation playback and speed control.
  • Auto-play on Reveal: play pronunciation automatically when showing answers.

Fast reaction gameplay focused on spotting vocabulary matches as quickly as possible.

How to play

  • Watch the active pair of cards.
  • Tap quickly when you spot a valid match.
  • Correct snaps build score; mistakes cost momentum.
  • Keep pace with the timer to finish the round strongly.

Available settings

  • Speech: optional pronunciation playback when available.
  • Sound Effects: toggle hit/miss feedback.
  • Topic Selection: choose active vocabulary topics from the Topics screen.
